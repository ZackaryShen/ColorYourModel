The reported symptom: clicking a red sapling marker never erases it. The cause is a coordinate-space split. Seeds are stored model-local — the centroid of the clicked triangle, read straight out of meshData.vertices (STL layout, Z-up). The mesh and the seed markers live in a scene group rotated −π/2 about X, so world = (x, z, −y) while every raycast and projection is in world space. The eraser's threshold is seedMarkerSize × 2.5 world units (a few pixels at the fitted zoom), and the billboard copies the camera's world quaternion into a child of the rotated group, so the glyph is drawn 90° off too.
